Notes

  1. Status stepper: Not Claimed → Claim Sent → Claim Received → Settled. A rejected claim shows a red banner with the reason and a “Reopen” button.
  2. Next-action card changes with each stage: submit, then follow up (with a reminder if overdue), then record credit note, then done.
  3. History is an audit trail of every status change, edit, upload, credit note and note, showing who did it and when.
  4. Settlement card shows the claim amount, how much is credited, a progress bar and the linked credit notes.
  5. Status changes open a short dialog that records the date, method and reference.

Flow map: the five demo workflows

1 · Create claim: New Claim → save → Claim Detail → appears in Claims table as Not Claimed.

2 · Change status: Claim Detail → Mark as sent (dialog) → Mark as received (dialog). Each step is logged in History.

3 · Follow SOP: Claim Detail → Follow SOP → Supplier SOP checklist (claim pre-selected) → shortcuts.

4 · Email draft: Claim Detail or SOP → Email Draft (pre-filled) → copy / mail app → Mark as sent.

5 · Credit note: Credit Notes or Claim Detail → Record credit note (dialog) → allocate → outstanding updates → Settled automatically.
